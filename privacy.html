Privacy Policy

Doors @ · Last updated August 27, 2026

Doors @ ("the app") is a show and festival tracking app. This page explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and who to contact with questions. Doors @ is operated by an individual developer, not a company. This policy describes actual practice, not boilerplate.

Information We Collect

When you create an account and use Doors @, we collect:

  • Account information: your email address, a display name, and a password. The password is stored as a one-way hash, so we never store or see your actual password.
  • Show history you log: artist name, date, venue, genre tags, and whether you have marked a show as a favorite.
  • Festival data: which festivals you mark as attending, your personal schedule picks, and membership in any crew or group you join or create with other users.
  • Approximate location, when available: a coarse, city-level location derived from your device's network connection at the moment you log a show, used only to help pre-fill a show's location. This is not precise GPS location, and it is not collected continuously, only at the moment of that action.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide the app's own features: building your personal show history, powering festival planning and crew coordination, and generating your year-end recap and poster. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with data brokers.

Third-Party Services

Doors @ sends a small amount of data to two third-party services, only as part of the features below, and never your account details, email, or password:

  • Last.fm: when you log a show, the artist name you typed is sent to Last.fm's public API to suggest a spelling correction and genre tags.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): when reviewing a logged show, the venue name you typed can be sent to OpenStreetMap's geocoding service to look up the venue's city.

Data Storage and Security

Doors @ is self-hosted on infrastructure operated directly by the developer, not a large third-party cloud platform. Passwords are stored hashed (bcrypt), never in plain text. All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your account and associated data, email us at the address below and we will confirm once it is done.

Children's Privacy

Doors @ is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
