Common Questions

How do I log a show?
From the Log tab, type it in plain language, something like "Turnstile at Metro on July 12", and the app fills in the artist, venue, and date on its own.
A show is stuck as "pending". What do I do?
Visit the Review tab. Every logged show gets a quick once-over there to confirm the artist name and genre before it is added to your permanent record.
How do I get my year-end recap poster?
Open the Recap tab and select "Make the Poster". It renders your whole year as a dated log, with any festival you attended nested inline with its full lineup.
Can I delete my account and data?
Yes, right from the app: open Account from the Log tab, then Delete Account. It's immediate and permanent, and it removes your logged shows, festival attendance, crew memberships, and any crews you created. You can also email ry.handyware@gmail.com from the address on your account if you'd rather we do it for you.
Is my data sold or shared?
No. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is collected and why.
